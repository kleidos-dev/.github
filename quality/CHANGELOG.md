# Quality kit changelog

One entry per release; each says what can turn a green repository red. The rules, and how a repository takes a release, are in [`docs/quality.md`](../docs/quality.md).

## 3.3.0 (2026-11-25)

Two decisions of the owner of 2026-11-25: no e-mail address in the fields that say who a thing comes from, and where a force push is allowed. MINOR, by [`docs/quality.md`, "Versions"](../docs/quality.md#versions): a rule every repository already passes. The impact preview, run on the `main` of boards, brand, cla, agents, videos, qa and meter, finds in each what 3.2.0 finds, and nothing of the new rule. What can turn red is a later change that puts an address in such a field.

- The e-mail rule, which read copyright lines and license texts, reads metadata too ([`supplier_fields.py`](supplier_fields.py), [`docs/quality.md`, "Supplier fields"](../docs/quality.md#supplier-fields)): the supplier and originator of `REUSE.toml`, the authors and maintainers of a manifest, the people of a citation or of a software bill of materials. One list names the files and fields, a `mailto:` link is read anywhere in them, and an address is searched for decoded and without the characters that do not show. `mail@kleidos.dev` is the one address that may stand there, with a waiver (`tool = "supplier"`, `what = "address:<field>"`) whose reason names the tool that needs it; any other address has no waiver. A file that cannot be read as its format says is searched whole; a file `REUSE.toml` states is a third party's, by an annotation of its own, is left alone, and so are the people of the packages a software bill of materials lists. The header check refuses an e-mail in a `SPDX-FileContributor` line.
- A force push is `git push --force-with-lease` on the branch of one's own pull request, and anything else only when the owner orders that act: the organization rules ([`agents/block.md`](agents/block.md)), [`docs/quality.md`, "Merging"](../docs/quality.md#merging), [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the comment of the commit-message gate, which said that only `main` is protected, now say so.
